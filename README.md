# Jerry Lee

Founder of **[ObjectRemover](https://objectremover.video/)**, a browser tool for removing unwanted objects, people, and on-screen text from videos and photos you own or are allowed to edit.

You describe what to remove in plain words, or brush over it, confirm the selection, and ObjectRemover rebuilds the background across frames.

**What it handles**
- Objects: logos on props, clutter, mics, signs
- People: passers-by and photobombers in the background
- Burned-in text: hardcoded captions, timestamps, name tags, stickers and emoji
- Cleanup of AI-generated video

**Where it runs**
- In the browser: [objectremover.video](https://objectremover.video/)
- In Adobe Premiere Pro 25.6+: [Premiere Pro plugin](https://objectremover.video/plugins/premiere-pro)

Docs, use-case guides, limits, and FAQ: [objectremover-docs](https://github.com/jerryleemaker/objectremover-docs)
